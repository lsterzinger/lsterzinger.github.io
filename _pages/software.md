I am involved in a couple open source software projects. 

## PyRAMS

I maintain a Python package called [PyRAMS](https://pyrams.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) - a collection of tools to more easily work with the [RAMS atmospheric research model's](https://vandenheever.atmos.colostate.edu/vdhpage/rams.php) output. 

* [Documentation](https://pyrams.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
* [GitHub/Source](https://github.com/lsterzinger/pyrams)

## Kerchunk
I am also involved in the Kerchunk software project, which allows for cloud-performant reading of NetCDF/HDF/GRIB/GeoTIFF/FITS data through [xarray](https://docs.xarray.dev/en/stable/) and [Zarr](https://zarr.readthedocs.io/en/stable/)

* [Documentation](https://fsspec.github.io/kerchunk/)
* [GitHub/Source](https://github.com/fsspec/kerchunk)
* [Medium blog post describing Kerchunk and how to use it](https://medium.com/pangeo/fake-it-until-you-make-it-reading-goes-netcdf4-data-on-aws-s3-as-zarr-for-rapid-data-access-61e33f8fe685)
* [Interactive tutorial I wrote for ESIP 2022 Winter Meeting](https://github.com/lsterzinger/2022-esip-kerchunk-tutorial)
